Furlenco Service Journey Mapping

One real service request, end to end

A wobbly coffee table reported through the Furlenco app on a Tuesday night. Confirmed in one minute. Auto-cancelled by Wednesday lunch - with the table still wobbly.

Tue, 8:04 PM
Request raised
Reported wobbly legs on the coffee table through the app. PCID created at 20:04.
Tue, 8:05 PM
Confirmed in 1 minute
'Thank you, request placed' plus a trackable PCID. Email at 8:06 PM, WhatsApp at 8:06 PM.
Tue, 11:36 PM
'In progress'
WhatsApp says the request is in progress and we'll be notified once scheduled.
Wed, 10:58 AM
Call from an unknown number
A personal, unregistered number called. Nothing identified it as Furlenco, so the call was not picked up.
Wed, 10:59 AM
A personal SMS
'This is Ram form Furlenco service team kindly call back once you free' - no PCID, no context.
Wed, afternoon
Finally connected
Spoke to the agent and asked for the visit on the weekend, as nobody would be home on a weekday.
Wed, afternoon
Weekend not possible
Told the request must be closed within 24 hours, so a weekend visit could not be booked against it.
Wed, 12:26 PM
Auto-cancelled
Request cancelled by the system. Email and WhatsApp both say: raise a new one if the issue persists.
Thu / Fri
Forced to restart
To get a weekend visit, the customer must raise a brand-new request on Thursday night or Friday - repeating the whole form.

What this journey tells us

Five things worth fixing, in the order they hurt the customer.

The 24-hour closure rule fights the customer's calendar
A policy meant to show speed instead forces cancellation when the customer simply isn't free on a weekday. Fix: let the customer pick a slot up to 7 days out and hold the request open against that slot.
Agents call from unregistered personal numbers
An unbranded number, identified only by Truecaller, is why the first call was missed. Fix: route all service calls through a verified Furlenco business number with the PCID in the SMS.
The system optimises for closing tickets, not solving problems
Status went Created -> Response Awaited -> Cancelled without the issue ever being touched. Fix: measure 'issue resolved', not 'request closed within 24 hours'.
The photo step can hard-block the request
A 10 MB limit with no compression stops customers at the final question. Fix: compress client-side and accept gallery uploads.
Good comms are wasted on a bad outcome
Email and WhatsApp were fast, branded and clear - and used to deliver a cancellation nobody asked for. Fix: never auto-cancel without an explicit customer confirmation.
